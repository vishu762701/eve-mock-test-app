Privacy Policy for Eve

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Eve ("the App", "we", "us") is a free mock test / exam preparation app for Government and entrance exams in India. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account, via Firebase Authentication.
  • Test Attempts: We store your exam attempts, scores, selected answers, and attempt dates so you can view your test history.
  • Device / Notification Token: To send you notifications about new exams or daily reminders, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token linked to your account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To let you sign in and identify your account.
  • To show your personal test history, scores, and progress.
  • To send optional notifications (new exams, daily reminders) if you enable them.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging). We do not run our own servers and do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with anyone except our infrastructure provider (Google Firebase) which processes data on our behalf under its own privacy and security policies.

Your Choices

  • You can turn off notifications any time from within the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for students preparing for competitive exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
